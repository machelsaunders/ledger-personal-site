This policy describes Ledger Personal, a native Mac and iPhone application built for its owner’s personal use.

Google account and Gmail access

Google’s native sign-in SDK requests basic account identity and the Gmail read-only scope. Ledger uses your email address to identify the connected mailbox. It searches billing-related messages and retrieves message IDs, dates, sender and subject headers, and short snippets. It stores these details and links to the source messages so you can review subscriptions and bills. It does not retrieve full email bodies or attachments in the current automatic scan.

The granted read-only scope permits mailbox reading; the app cannot send, delete or label messages through this connection. Email content is treated as evidence and never as an instruction to make a payment or cancel a service.

Financial records

Ledger stores the subscription, bill, expense, reminder and account snapshot information you enter or confirm. These records may include amounts, billing dates, payment account labels, cancellation dates and email evidence. Manually entered balances are snapshots. An optional private investment-service connection can read brokerage balances, holdings, research and paper-trading results. Virtual funds are kept separate from actual account balances.

Investment-service credentials stay in the device Keychain and are not included in iCloud sync. Remote investment access uses a private HTTPS service reachable through the owner's Tailscale network. Ledger's reader connection has no trading or transfer controls.

Storage and optional iCloud sync

Records are saved locally in an application support JSON file with restricted file permissions and a previous-save backup. The JSON file is not encrypted by Ledger itself. OAuth credentials are stored using Google’s SDK and the device Keychain.

If you enable iCloud sync, Ledger sends financial records, audit details, mailbox identity and review snippets to Apple CloudKit’s private database for your Apple Account. This lets your Mac and iPhone share the same inventory and review decisions. OAuth tokens and Gmail scan continuation cursors are not included in that sync. Apple and Google operate their respective services under their own privacy policies.

Use and sharing

Google user data is used only to provide Ledger’s billing discovery, review and optional device synchronization features. Ledger does not sell this data, use it for advertising, send it to an AI service, or use it to train models. It has no app analytics or hosted ingestion backend in this build. Exporting a backup creates a file containing private financial records, under your control.

Ledger Personal’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your controls and retention

You can disconnect Gmail in Connections or revoke Ledger’s access through your Google Account connections. Disconnecting stops future access and retains your reviewed records. Pausing iCloud sync stops future synchronization and leaves existing records intact.

Records remain until you remove them. Dismissed or consumed email candidates retain their message ID to prevent rediscovery; synced removals retain an identifier and deletion marker. To erase the entire workspace, disable sync, remove the application’s local data and backups on every device, and remove Ledger’s private data through your Apple Account’s iCloud storage controls. Removing the app alone may leave data or Keychain credentials behind. Independently exported backups must be removed separately.

Notifications and service artwork

Local reminders can show service names, amounts, payment account labels, billing dates and cancellation or expiry details. Your device's notification-preview and Focus settings control their visibility. Existing service artwork may be fetched from Apple's public image CDN while reminders are prepared; bill details are not added to artwork requests. If artwork is unavailable, the app uses a colored service symbol. Artwork is prepared locally before delivery.

This public website

This website contains only application information and these policies. No financial records, email evidence or credentials are published here. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may log visitor IP addresses for security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site has no forms, analytics scripts or advertising.

Contact and changes

For questions about Ledger Personal’s data use, contact the support address displayed on its Google consent screen. If the application’s data handling changes, this policy and its update date will change before new handling is enabled.
