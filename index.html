ledger Personal
Mac & iPhone · Personal use

A little clarity.
Every day.

Ledger Personal keeps recurring subscriptions, bills, payment dates and cancellation reminders together. Review the evidence behind a charge, track expenses and keep account balance snapshots in a private workspace.

Know what’s coming up.

Connect Gmail with read-only authorization to discover billing emails. New messages enter a review queue; you verify the amount, payment account and next date before treating them as confirmed commitments.

Optional private iCloud sync shares records between your Mac and iPhone. Google authorization and notification permissions are set up on each device.

This is a personal development application. Optional investment access reads account snapshots from a privately connected service. Ledger does not make payments, execute trades or cancel services automatically.
