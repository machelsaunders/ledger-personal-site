Ledger Personal
Updated October 11, 2026

A personal workspace
for your finances.

Ledger Personal is a development application for its owner's private use. This website describes that application; it does not offer public registration, sell a subscription or accept payments.

Your records and connections

Connect only accounts you own or are authorized to access. Review email evidence, amounts, payment sources and dates before confirming a record. An inferred or manually entered renewal date is an estimate until verified with the billing provider.

Reminders and service availability

Reminders depend on device permissions, Focus settings and the stored billing details. Synchronization depends on internet access, the connected account and application availability. Check important cancellation deadlines with the provider directly.

Financial actions

Ledger organizes information and reads optional account snapshots. It does not execute payments, transfers, trades or subscription cancellations. Paper-trading figures describe a simulation and are separate from real assets.

Privacy and support

See the Privacy policy for storage, Google access and optional iCloud synchronization. For support, use the support address displayed on Ledger Personal's Google consent screen.
